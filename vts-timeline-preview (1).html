VTS Timeline — kconcat-style redesign (interactive preview)

This is what the #tlTools rail + .tl-head button row become when rebuilt the way kconcat/src/crates/concat/ui/timeline/tray.slint does it: tabs → one 40px icon tray → lane grid with per-lane header switches. Video tracks are mixed media — video, image and caption clips share them, captions floating above same-track media — with audio at the bottom; the top lane is the top layer. Try it: zoom buttons, click a clip to select, R for the razor then click a clip to split it, Ctrl+K splits at the playhead, H pans, T drops a caption on the topmost video track, Alt+scroll over a lane header resizes it, pills filter the section strip, the + menu adds tracks, lane headers lock / hide / mute.

#tlTools rail → tray (6 tools)

razor (R)movedBecomes a tray toggle with the kconcat blade line (accent hairline where the cut lands). Shortcut stays R (kconcat uses B).
text (T)deletedNo click-on-timeline caption tool — kconcat has none. Replaced by + caption at playhead (T), dropped on the topmost video track.
hand (H)movedSame pan toggle, now in the tray next to trim-follow.
zoom (Z)deletedkconcat has no zoom tool: fit / − / + buttons plus Ctrl+wheel cover it. Try them above.
mute (M)deletedClick-a-clip-to-mute is gone. Audio lane headers get a mute switch; M mutes an audio lane, or toggles output on a video lane.

New in this redesign

mixed V tracksnewVideo tracks hold video + image + caption clips mixed together — a caption is just another media clip. Captions are transparent overlays, so they float above same-track media instead of colliding with it. No T lanes anywhere.
V / A groupsnewPremiere-style: just two groups — video on top, audio at the bottom. Top lane is the top layer: V2 above V1, A1 above A2.
lane headersnewPremiere-style typed headers: lock, output (eye) on video lanes, mute on audio lanes, kind letter, ··· menu with Delete — greyed on the last lane standing (track-header.slint). No height button: Alt+scroll over a header resizes that lane, double-click resets to auto.
tabs stripnewOne tab per sequence above the tray (tabs.slint). Starts with a single “Timeline 01”.
section pillsnewcaption / silence / other / all-silence / remove-selected move up from the List panel onto the tray, wired to the same filters. (Moving to a dedicated Sections panel next.)
section stripnew6px bars along the ruler’s foot; click toggles selection for removal.
merge (disabled)newGreyed with the reason in its tooltip — kconcat’s rule: a blocked button says why.
